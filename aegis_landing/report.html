This report presents the findings of Operation Nightfall (INV-8492), a critical-priority investigation initiated on October 14, 2023, by Dir. Vance. The investigation was prompted by intelligence indicating potential illicit activities linked to a shell company, Aegis Corp, operating out of the Pier District.

The AI-assisted analysis identified 247 entities and 518 relationships, leading to 7 actionable findings, of which 4 have been reviewed and approved by the lead investigator.

Key evidence includes a partial license plate match (7X-A4**) against watchlist WL-4491, a $14,200 wire transfer to an offshore account, and intercepted Signal communications referencing planned activities.
Cross-case analysis has revealed a connection to INV-8301 (Echo Syndicate), a previously closed investigation that may require re-evaluation. This report recommends escalation and additional surveillance.
